Add a custom call-to-action button to your LinkedIn Page

Last updated: 3 weeks ago

Who can use this feature?

We’re gradually making this feature available, and you might not have access to it at this time. You must be a super admin of the Page to subscribe to Premium Company Page. 

A custom call-to-action button can be used to boost engagement with your LinkedIn Page. If you’re a Page admin, you can choose different names for the call-to-action and customize it with a unique URL. When you create a custom call-to-action, the button will appear at the top of your Page. Any URL can be used for the call-to-action button you choose. 

Important to know

You can add Request services as a call-to-action button if you offer services on your Company Page. Learn more about offering services on LinkedIn on your Company Page. You can add Request demo as a call-to-action button only if you have a published Flagship product page. Learn more about Flagship product pages.

To add a call-to-action to your LinkedIn Page:

  1. Go to your Page super admin view.

  2. Click Edit page in the left menu.

  3. In the Buttons tab, turn on the Custom button toggle.

  4. Select the Button name.

  5. Enter the URL for the button.

  6. Optionally, you can turn on the Increase button visibility toggle.

  7. Click Save.

Important to know

If you don’t have a Premium Company Page subscription, the call-to-action option is only visible when members click the ﻿﻿ More button. 

Results

You can review the number of custom button clicks from your followers in Visitor Analytics after adding a call-to-action button to your Premium Company Page.
